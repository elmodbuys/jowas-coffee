Our Story

From a Karoo boy's dream to your cup.

Jowa's was founded in 2021 by Jowayne Van Wyk, the nickname "Jowa" was earned on the rugby field playing for the Springbok U19's in 2005. Jowa's was started with a simple idea: create something that brings people together from all walks of life. In 2026 "Jowa", a coffee lover himself, founded Jowa's Coffee with the same core idea that the Jowa's brand was launched with.

Every cup uses AAA-plus graded beans from Ethiopia, India, Burundi and Colombia - well known for a medium arabica with real body.
